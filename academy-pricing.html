How we got here — the Sept 11 comparables

Searched Sept 11. Individual, self-paced Christian study clusters tightly; the only thing priced like “$100 a month” is a facilitated cohort with a reading load of a book a month — which settled that the number was per year.

Memento (LCMS men's movement — Mark's model)Daily devotions, weekly small group of 4–8, an “anchor”; 4,000+ men$60 founders → $90
HallowCatholic prayer app; family plan $119.99 for six$69.99 ($9.99/mo)

1517 Academy · Hillsdale · BSFFree, donor-funded$0

Where the Academy sits. More than an app (a chapter that meets, a mission plan, a discussion leader) and less than Colson (no book-a-month, no 10-hour weeks). That is the $90–$180 band; $99 is inside it, above Memento so it doesn't look like the cheaper cousin, and still an easy yes.

Founding-member practice: 20–30% off is the usual guidance (Mark's 30% for new chapters lands on it; Boulder's 50% was promised first and is honored). Lock the rate for life, cap and date it, offer annual and monthly with the annual saving visible, and say what the money is for — one sentence: “your subscription funds the Academy's production and keeps it in LCRL's hands.”
