Why two editions

Same Truth. Two Voices.

Different settings call for different presence. The classical edition reads beautifully in a pew rack or church bulletin board. The modern edition holds its own in a corporate boardroom or campus event. Same story, same content — just dressed for the occasion.

01

One Brand System, Two Vocabularies

Both versions use Liberty Gold, Freedom Blue, Constitution Navy, and Gospel Light, with Bebas Neue, Poppins, and Playfair carrying every page. What differs is the visual posture — painterly vs. editorial.

02

Two Image Sets

v1 features painterly illustrations (Christ, scales of justice, two-kingdom diptych). v2 features modern editorial photography (a man in prayer, courthouse cross, Bible study around a table) generated with OpenAI's gpt-image-2.

03

True Print-Ready Output

Both built as HTML + CSS, rendered through Puppeteer with preferCSSPageSize. Pages are 8.75 × 11.25 in (US Letter trim plus 0.125" bleed) with crop marks rendered into the bleed.

Print Specifications

Built for the Press

Both editions share the same print specs. Hand either PDF to any North American print shop.

Trim Size

8.5 × 11 in

US Letter — the standard pew-rack and direct-mail format.

Bleed

0.125 in

Full bleed all four sides; full page measures 8.75 × 11.25 in.

Safe Margin

0.25 in

All text safely inside the trim. Total edge-to-text: 0.375 in.

Crop Marks

Included

Drawn into the bleed at all four corners, ready for the cutter.

Page Count

8 Pages

Two saddle-stitched sheets folded — one signature, no spine.

Color Mode

RGB → CMYK

Source built in RGB; ask your printer to convert to CMYK on output.

Image DPI

300 ppi

Hero imagery rendered at 1024 × 1536, downsampled cleanly to print scale.

Build Tool

Puppeteer

Headless Chromium with preferCSSPageSize. node print-build/build-pdf.js regenerates both PDFs.
