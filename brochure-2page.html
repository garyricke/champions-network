An Initiative of LCRL

The Champions Network.

Educating, inspiring, and encouraging two‑kingdom cultural servants.

A vibrant community learning, growing, praying, and challenging one another to engage their communities with two-kingdom principles.

Our Mission

We inspire and equip individuals dedicated to making a difference in their congregations, schools, and communities. By championing religious liberty for all — amplifying the LORD’s voice in the left-hand as well as the right-hand kingdom — we put our temporal liberties to work for the greater, eternal liberties of the Gospel.

Two-Kingdom Doctrine

Two kingdoms, one mission.

Our culture keeps chipping away at America’s most basic religious liberties, despite the First Amendment. For the sake of our communities and the church, we must engage — on God’s terms. God engages the world in two ways.

Left-Hand Kingdom

To Preserve It

God works through us as parents, citizens, and cultural servants to preserve the foundational principles that order and protect a common life.

Right-Hand Kingdom

To Save It

God sent His Son and the power of His Word to save us. We carry the message of salvation and the freedom found in Christ alone.

To bless the world now and forever.

Who We Are

Advocates

What We Offer

The Champions Academy.

A one-, two-, or three-year set of educational resources for learning, discussing, praying, and engaging others in a humble but deliberate way. Each group in the Network is a Champions Chapter.

10

Sessions a Year

Resources are batched into session themes. Chapters meet at least monthly — many meet more often — adapting each session to their people.

2

Ways to Learn

A Group Discussion Guide with tutorial videos and handouts, plus Home Study Guides so each participant grows on their own, a little each day.

1

Community Action Plan

Through the first year, every chapter builds a plan and takes on achievable objectives — listening and praying before engaging.

The Toolchest

Every chapter is equipped.

Toolkit

Educational resources plus the communication apps that keep chapters in touch with Network staff — and with facilitators of other chapters.

Connections

Chapter leaders talk with one another state to state, plus networking with state and federal legislators through our connections on The Hill.

Facilitators

Four to five chapter facilitators are enlisted with the Network Director and the congregation’s pastor, so many hands make light work.

Academy Outcomes

Seeking to be useful in God’s hands.

  • Differentiate the two kingdoms — discerning how God may be at work in the left-hand kingdom.
  • Engage with humble restraint — vocationally respectful, listening and praying first.
  • Be constitutionally astute — equipped to take the politics off the issues.
  • Speak to life, marriage, and education — the freedoms most at stake.
How to Get Involved

Host a Champions Discovery Weekend.

Invite an LCRL staff member to your church for a Saturday morning and Sunday afternoon introduction workshop, with preaching and Bible class options. Membership is open to anyone willing to commit to a chapter for at least a year.
