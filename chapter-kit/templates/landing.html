For Neighboring Congregations

Stand with the students

A university campus is a lonely place to hold a biblical conviction — in the lecture hall, in the dorm, in conversations our students didn't choose.

A student's calendar is rarely their own. A chapter made only of students empties out at midterms, and the handful still in the room lose heart. Your steady presence is what makes it hold.

  • Support the students. Be the ones who show up every time, so the students are never the only ones in the room.
  • Get equipped yourself. Two-kingdom thinking, the First Amendment, life, marriage, and education.
  • Bring it home. What you learn here, you carry back into your own congregation.
For CU Students

You're the host

You already know the moment: a class discussion turns, and you have about four seconds to decide whether to say anything. This chapter is where you get ready for that moment — before it comes.

  • Learn what you actually believe — and why the First Amendment protects it.
  • Learn how to say it without being the person nobody wants to sit next to.
  • Invite your congregation. Bring members of area congregations with you — they're coming for you as much as for themselves.
