Who This Chapter Is For

Two invitations, one table.

St. Stephen Lutheran Church is the home of this chapter — but the room was never meant to stop at the property line, and that is by design.

For the St. Stephen Congregation

Get equipped together

You have had the conversation — at work, at a family table, in a school parking lot — and walked away wishing you had said it better. This chapter is where you get ready for the next one, before it comes.

Team Jesus already knows how to show up for one another. This is that same thing, pointed at the questions the culture is asking your family right now.

  • Learn what you actually believe — and why the First Amendment protects it.
  • Learn how to say it with clarity and charity, rather than heat.
  • Go together. A conviction is easier to hold when the people in the next row are holding it too.
For Neighboring Congregations

Bring your congregation

This chapter meets at St. Stephen, but it was never meant to stop at the property line. Congregations across the Liberty area are welcome at the table.

You do not need to be a member here, and you do not need to arrive knowing anything. You need an evening a month and a willingness to show up.

  • Come as you are. No cost, no prior study, and no need to be a member of St. Stephen.
  • Get equipped yourself. Two-kingdom thinking, the First Amendment, life, marriage, and education.
  • Bring it home. What you learn here, you carry back into your own congregation.
By doing good you should silence the ignorant talk of foolish people. 1 Peter 2:15
