Letting Doctrinal Review approve a page

Step 4 is the one gate nobody on our side controls. On September 11 the question got sharper: Mark is taking it to the President’s office, and Gary described two ways to build it if they say yes.

Settled: send them in batches

Mark gives Doctrinal Review three or four sessions at a time. One wait per batch instead of one per session.

Option A — the simple one

A gated page with the session on it and, at the bottom, a text box: type your notes, press submit. Gary (and Mark, or Shelly) get the notes instantly. Nothing to download, no email to compose. Reads like a page; works on a phone.

Option B — notes against the exact spot

The same page, but the reviewer can highlight a passage and leave a note on it, the way Mark asked: “can they mark it up?” Every note arrives tied to the sentence it’s about. Gary has built this for another project. Either option can add an Approve button.

  • Opens on a phone or a computer, nothing to download
  • Approved changes are applied once — the page is the document, so PDF and site can’t drift
  • Older reviewers who want paper can still get the PDF; nothing is taken away
Mark’s route: he can’t ask the reviewers (anonymous by design), so he’s asking the assistant to the President, copying Georgia, and they can carry it to the board if it needs that. His read from the hymn review: the comment back was “it’d be nice to see the whole thing” — which a page does better than a PDF. Frame it as fewer steps for them.

If the answer is no, nothing is lost — the PDF path already works and stays.
