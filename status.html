Subscriber / Paid Academy — Build Plan

Mark greenlit building the paid Academy shell now. Below is what was decided on the call and the open work to stand it up. Target launch: end of summer 2026.

What was decided
  • Charge up front. Yes to taking money on signup — but pair it with discounting so people still grasp the value.
  • Coupon codes for free / beta / discounted access — useful for beta-testing on a first cohort without giving up a posted price.
  • Recurring billing — monthly or annual, with the annual plan discounted.
  • Gift subscriptions — a donor can buy seats for a church (Mark's example: $1,000 covers ~20 subscribers). "Memento does that."
  • Gated "Subscriber Academy" tab alongside Two Kingdoms · Journey · Academy. Sessions 2 & 3 stay public for now; new uploads go behind the gate.
Admin backend
  • Give Mark a back-admin to grant a membership by email on someone's behalf — decoupled from the Stripe transaction.
  • Covers donors who give through the St. Louis donation office (the main-page "donut" donation goes there, not to a Champions merchant account) — grant access without tying it to the payment.
Compliance & money plumbing
  • LCRL is a 501(c)(3); payments need a bank account designated for that entity locked into the Stripe setup.
  • Mark to check with St. Louis on protocols before standing up the merchant account — confirm no "landmines."
  • Gary to write internal docs on the Stripe setup + how to start taking money.
  • Launch also needs a board oversight report (per prior planning).
Build sequence
  • 1 Stripe account + designated 501(c)(3) bank account MarkGary docs
  • 2 Subscriber-gated Academy shell + new nav tab Gary
  • 3 Checkout (monthly/annual) + coupon codes + gift subscriptions Gary
  • 4 Admin backend (grant access by email) Gary
