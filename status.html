It covered two things — posting podcast teasers and helping the Champions Network with its marketing — but the marketing half was never scoped: no deliverable list, no boundary, no share of the hours set aside for it. The 60 hours were sized against the podcast work — 60 hours across Mar–Aug at $100/hr. What it has actually funded is an Academy platform build. The overrun is a scope question, not a pace problem.

So one JSON file per chapter now drives everything — node print-build/build-chapter-kit.js <slug> emits the poster PDF, a 2-up half-sheet PDF (adult sheet + student sheet), 1080² and 1080×1350 social PNGs, a QR, and a live landing page. He wrote that he wasn't sure how to phrase the invitation: the students host the chapter but can't sustain it alone — schedules collapse at midterms and a half-empty room demoralizes the rest. The pieces order the two motives rather than blending them: neighboring-congregation adults are asked to come for the students first, for their own training second. Adult-facing headline "Stand with the students who are standing for their faith"; student-facing "Hold your ground. You don't have to hold it alone."
  • Landing page + RSVP: /boulder carries the full schedule and an RSVP form. Every chapter posts to one Netlify form (chapter-rsvp) with hidden chapter fields. Outstanding: Carly must be added as an email notification on that form in the Netlify dashboard — until then submissions are captured but nobody is told.
  • Review page: new gated /chapters in the admin panel shows every chapter's pieces with thumbnails and downloads, and regenerates on each build so new chapters appear on their own.
  • Build guard added. The kit builder measures every fixed-height print box and exits non-zero rather than shipping a clipped PDF. It caught the poster body running 121px long — and both social posts overflowing silently, because scrollHeight omits a container's own padding-bottom. The guard now also measures last-child position against the padding box.
