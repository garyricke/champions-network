The State of Things

Christianity Criminalized? It’s true.

Christianity is being culturally challenged, maligned, and rebuffed by a growing group of people and groups in America. While Christians simply want to be kind and win souls for Jesus, we are vilified instead — for believing in something larger than government and its leaders.

What progressive humanists are saying

  • The Bible promotes “hate”
  • Parental authority prevents progress
  • The government is greater than God

Fundamental rights at stake

  • Religious Freedoms
  • Marriage as Institution
  • Sanctity of Life
  • Say in Children’s Education

We cannot let these freedoms die or America as we know it will die as well. We need champions in the church who will stand up for our God-given rights as citizens. With God, there is a better way.

Two-Kingdom Doctrine

Engaging in Culture God’s way.

God provides the way for Christians to engage the world through Scripture and reason — the same way He engages us. He preserves the world for a time so that He may save those who believe in Him.

Left-Hand Kingdom

Champion Cultural Servants

God can use us as parents, citizens, law enforcement, governing agencies, etc., to preserve the foundation principles of America. We must work to preserve our culture as founded on Biblical principles.

God Preserves
Right-Hand Kingdom

Champion Witnesses

God sent His Son Jesus and the power of His Word through His Spirit to save us from our sinful nature and its negative impact on our world. We work to spread the message of salvation in Jesus and the freedom found in Him.

God Saves
The Network

Becoming a Champion.

Seeing the need for cultural servants to link arms and become a stronger voice in our culture, the Lutheran Center for Religious Liberty created the Champions for Religious Liberty Network — a passionate group of Champion Citizens working as a faithful solution to a powerful problem.

01

The Academy

Individuals and groups begin with the Champions Academy — learning together, supporting one another, and building plans of engagement.

02

The Church

Groups within churches participate in the Champions Network. We work with pastors and leadership to provide extra support for Champion Citizens.

03

The Network

The nationwide Champions Network connects Citizens and Churches community to community, state to state, coast to coast.
